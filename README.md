## My Favorite Series: Court of Gold

**Genre:** Sports documentary
**Year:** 2025

### Why I Like It

I like Court of Gold because the story of how the team USA 2024 win the gold medal is *really* engaging, you can actually see their effort and team chemistry even though they played against each other on the NBA for decades and years. My favorite part is when Kevin Durant says to their opponent which at the finals which was france that their chemistry is not gon help when they gotta guard Stephen Curry my goattt.

### Main Characters

- Stephen Curry
- Kevin Durant
- Lebron James

### My Top 3 Episodes

1. Episode 6 (Team USA relies on a late-game surge and a three-point barrage from Steph Curry to fend off France for the gold.)
2. Episode 1 (Team USA assembles a star-loaded roster from the NBA following a disappointing 2023 World Cup performance last year.)
3. Episode 3 (Coach Steve Kerr discusses the dynamics of managing an NBA MVP-laden roster)

### Favorite Quote
